# Tim Cope's Pro Skater (TCPS) for Godot 4.8+

Skateboarding for the [3D Player Controller](../3d_player_controller/README.md): a rideable board with the ground, air, vert, grind, balance and trick physics of Neversoft's Tony Hawk's Underground source, its combo score and the THPS camera. The board owns the movement, the sounds and the camera; the Player lends its body, its input and its animations through the controller's `Riding` state, which makes the board's camera current and turns the Player's step-up ray off while the board owns the ground. The board touches no Player flags and no Player UI.

> [!NOTE]
> Requires `addons/3d_player_controller` (the `Player`, its `Riding` state and the skateboarding animation clips in `player.tscn`) and, through it, [`addons/controls`](https://github.com/kirbycope/godot-controls), which is where `ActionPrompt` and the on-screen button hints live. `Skateboard` and `SkateboardCamera` register their class names on their own; enabling the plugin only adds the board to the Create New Node dialog.

## Interactive Demo Scene

Open and run **`res://addons/tcps/scenes/skate_park.tscn`**. The Player starts on the board (the scene's `MountTimer` calls the pickup board's `equip()` after 0.3 s). Hold Up to push (hold Ollie or Grab while pushing to crouch and push harder, THUG's held X) and Down to brake or turn sharp; hold Ollie and let go to pop (the longer the hold, the higher); in the air press Flip or Grab with a direction for the trick and hold Left/Right to spin; hold Grind over the ledge, the bench or the handrail (with a direction for the grind) and balance with Left/Right; tap Up then Down for a manual (Down then Up for a nose manual) and balance with Up/Down; let go of push on a transition to go vert, or hold it at the lip to fly over the deck; hold Grind going up past a coping for a lip trick, balance with Up/Down and pop off (Up held jumps onto the deck, Left/Right drop back in along the coping); hold Grind into the concrete wall in the air to ride it, or press Ollie as you hit it square for a wallplant; hold Revert rising on the spine to transfer to its far face, or in the air off the quarter pipe's deck (up the bank behind it) to acid drop in; hold Up behind the truck on its road to skitch, balance with Left/Right and let go with Down or a pop; land clean to bank the combo, or Revert on a vert landing and manual out to keep it; K (D-pad Down) gets off with the board in hand, and on foot K gets back on, on the ground or in the air, while Revert on foot jumps onto the board to acid drop into a ramp ahead.

| Node | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Ground` (`CSGBox3D`, group `CONCRETE`) | The flat park floor; the group picks the concrete roll sound. |
| `HalfPipe/LeftTransition`, `RightTransition` (`CSGPolygon3D`, group `WOOD`) | A 3 m radius quarter circle in 3 degree facets with 0.3 m of vert and a 2 m deck, extruded 8 m; the right one is the same polygon rotated 180 degrees. `LeftCoping` / `RightCoping` are decorative, `Flat` is the plywood between them. |
| `QuarterPipe`, `Funbox` (`CSGPolygon3D`, group `WOOD`) | A lone transition and a kicker with a table top. |
| `Ledge`, `Bench` (`CSGBox3D`) and `Handrail` (three `CSGCylinder3D`) | Things to grind. Each carries `Rail` children (`Path3D` with `rail.gd`) along its grindable edges: both long edges of the ledge and the bench, the top of the handrail. |
| `Wall` (`CSGBox3D`, group `CONCRETE`) | A slab to bounce off. |
| `Player` | `player.tscn`, nothing to set. |
| `Skateboard` | The pickup board (`skateboard.tscn`); `skate_park.gd` equips it through the same path the prompt uses. |

## How to Use

| Node | Where it goes | Set in the Inspector |
|---|---|---|
| `Skateboard` (instance `scenes/skateboard.tscn`) | On the ground anywhere in your level | Nothing required; the surface groups `WOOD`, `STONE` / `COBBLESTONE` and `CONCRETE` on your floors pick the roll sounds |
| `SkateboardCamera` (inside `skateboard.tscn`) | Nothing to add | `behind`, `above`, `tilt`, `field_of_view`, the per-sixtieth `slerp` and `lerp` fractions (plain, vert air, landed), `grind_zoom`, `lookaround_max` |
| `Rail` (a `Path3D` with `scripts/rail.gd`) | Along any edge the board should grind, as a child of the ledge or rail it belongs to, with the curve's points on the edge; along the coping of a vert wall it is also where lip tricks happen | `speed_boost` (m/s added on locking on), `leave_angle_degrees` (a sharper bend ends the rail) |
| A wall in the `wallride` node group | Any collider the board may ride: the group is the only mark, so a `CSGBox3D` or a `StaticBody3D` in it is a wallride wall, one not in it is a wall to bonk off or wallplant | |
| A vehicle in the `skitchable` node group | Anything the board may hang off the back of, with a `SkitchPoint` child (a `Marker3D`) at the spot the skater hangs from, or the node itself when there is none; the board reads the vehicle's velocity off that point's movement, so any moving node will do (the park's truck is a `StaticBody3D` under a `PathFollow3D` with `scripts/path_mover.gd`, and a `Vehicle` from the gta addon needs only the group and the marker) | `path_mover.gd`: `speed` (m/s along the path) |

Looking at the board and pressing Action mounts it: `Skateboard.equip()` calls `Player.mount()`, the `Riding` state calls back `mount(player)`, and from then on the board's `ride(player, delta)` moves the Player every physics tick (it drives the `CharacterBody3D` and the Player's movement API: velocity, floor settings, `orientation`, `model_pitch`, the model turn helpers and `update_movement_and_rotation`), `ride_input(player, event)` handles the ollie (charged on press, popped on release), the grind button, the kick push, the manual taps and the dismount through the board's own action exports (resolved for the `input_type` the state keeps current), and `camera` is the current camera. `state` says which of THUG's states the skater is in (`GROUND`, `AIR`, `RAIL`, `LIP` on a coping, `WALL` riding a wall), `trick` and `balance` which balance trick is on, `air_trick` which flip or grab is in the air, and `tricks` (a `SkateTricks`) holds the combo, the score and the special meter (`special`, `special_lit`); `trick_started` / `trick_ended(kind, bailed)`, `combo_banked(points)` and `combo_lost` say when those change. The `HUD` in `skateboard.tscn` shows the rider the balance meter, the combo line with its worth, and the score. K (or D-pad Down, the `dismount` action exports) calls `Player.dismount()` with the board kept in hand: the combo is banked, `carrier_peer` is set (replicated like `rider_peer`), the board hangs from the `RightHand` bone of the carrier's skeleton (or at the hip of a model without one) on every peer, its pickup area is off, and the Player's own camera returns; the same action on foot, or a revert (spine) button, calls `Player.mount()` again, in the air too, and a spine button on the ground adds THUG's `acid_drop_jump_velocity` first. The same rider keeps the run's score across walking; a new rider starts a new one. A board nobody carries (placed in a scene, or left by a rider who disconnected) is still mounted by looking at it and pressing Action. `get_contextual_controls(input_type)` names the labels (`"joypad_button_3": "Ollie"`), and the board's `GroundRay` picks the roll sound.

Animations are the Player's: the board asks for them through `locomotion_requested("SkateboardingLocomotion" / "SkateboardingKickPush")`, `locomotion_blend_requested`, and `jump_requested`, which the `Riding` state connects to the Player's AnimationTree.

The board's own animations are in `skateboard.tscn`: the mesh hangs off a `Board` pivot, and the scene's `AnimationPlayer` turns that pivot (`Board:rotation`) with one animation per trick, named as the trick is in snake case (`ollie`, `kickflip`, `heelflip`, `pop_shove_it`, `impossible`, `hardflip`, `varial_kickflip`, `varial_heelflip`, `inward_heelflip`, `sal_flip`; an extra replays its base flip's; `Skateboard.animation_name_for("Pop Shove-It")` gives `pop_shove_it`). A pop plays `ollie`, a flip trick plays its own, and each flip lasts `SkateTricks.FLIP_TIME`; landing or taking a rail stops whatever is playing and puts the pivot back level. To animate a new trick, add an animation of that name to the player and it plays; a trick with no animation of its own leaves the board still.

Over the network the board is one node in the world on every peer, and its `BodySynchronizer` (`resources/skateboard_replication.tres`) carries its position, rotation and `rider_peer`, the peer whose Player is on it (0 when it is free). Getting on asks the server to put the board under the rider's model on every peer and then hand it to the rider's peer, in that order, so the rider's copy starts sending its place only once everybody has it under their feet; getting off does the same back to the parent it stood under and to the server. A rider who drops out is handed back by every peer on its own, and a board another peer is riding refuses a second rider. Offline there is nobody to ask and the hand-off is immediate.

## Physics and camera

Ported from the THUG source at https://github.com/thug1src/thug, state by state; `docs/thug_skater_reference.md` in the repository is the digest of the skater code, with the THUG symbol behind every tunable and, in its table of `physics.q` values, the numbers the game ships with (from the decompiled scripts at https://github.com/atljp/thps-modding-resources). The constants at the top of `skateboard.gd`, `skate_balance.gd` and `skateboard_camera.gd` are those numbers turned from inches, feet and milliseconds into metres and seconds, at the middle of each stat range: gravity is THUG's 3.5 g in the air, a full ollie pops at 11 m/s, a standing push reaches 11.3 m/s and a crouched one (sprint) 15.3, the ground turns at 1.8 radians a second, a rail is taken from a metre away with 3.8 m/s added. THUG's own 8 to 12 ft vert ramps are what a standing push clears; the demo's 3.3 m half pipe wants the crouched push.

- **Ground** (`do_on_ground_physics`): every tick the velocity is rotated into the surface with its speed kept (`RotateToPlane`, never a projection), gravity along the surface slows a climb, and the board never slides: a turn rotates the velocity with the facing at a constant rate whatever the speed (sharper with Down), the speed is put back along the facing (`remove_sideways_velocity`), and rolling backwards turns the skater round. A kick (Up) is a steady acceleration to a cap, Down brakes, and the only friction is the wind, quadratic in speed and heavy above the soft cap. A wall is bounced off (`bounce_off_wall`): the board runs along it and loses speed by how square-on the hit was, to nothing head-on.
- **Ollie** (`do_jump`): charged while the button is down and popped when it comes up, its speed linear in the hold up to half a second, a downward speed thrown away first; a smaller range off vert; off a rail it leaves the rail.
- **Air** (`do_in_air_physics`): Left/Right spin once held past a tap, then ramp to full rate; leaving a wall steeper than 50 degrees is a vert launch, with the velocity rotated into the wall's vertical plane and the skater held in that plane while the wall is still behind them, coming back down onto it; holding forward at the lip breaks vert and flies over the deck; a landing turns the skater to face the way they roll.
- **Rails** (`CRailManager::StickToRail`, `got_rail`, `do_rail_physics`): looked for only in the air with Grind held, along the tick's move, within `RAIL_MAX_SNAP`, with a rail the board travels along scoring eight times better than one across it. Locking on puts the horizontal speed along the rail plus the rail's boost; gravity along the rail speeds a descent and turns a stall round; the rail ends at its last point or a bend past its leave angle, and an ollie leaves it. No rail is taken again for a moment after leaving one.
- **Balance** (`CManual`, `SkateBalance`): one model for manuals and grinds. The needle leans further the further it leans, faster the longer the trick lasts; the buttons push it back (Left/Right on a rail, Up/Down in a manual); left alone it drifts and never rests; off either end is a bail, which takes most of the speed and the controls for a moment.
- **Tricks and the score** (`CTrickComponent`, `Score` in `Code/Sk/Modules/Skate/score.cpp`, the trick scripts, `SkateTricks`): a flip (Square in THUG) or a grab (Circle) in the air is named by the direction held, one of eight (the diagonals when two are held), from tables at the top of `skate_tricks.gd` that are THUG's created skater's own slots (`CustomTricks_default` in `protricks.q`): flips `Kickflip`, `Heelflip`, `Impossible`, `Pop Shove-It`, `Hardflip`, `Inward Heelflip`, `Varial Kickflip`, `Varial Heelflip`; grabs `Melon`, `Indy`, `Nosegrab`, `Tailgrab`, `Japan`, `Madonna`, `Benihana`, `Airwalk`; a grind is named the same way when the rail is taken, from `GrindTrickList`: along the travel `50-50`, `Nosegrind`, `5-0`, `Tailslide`, `Noseslide`, `Crooked`, `Overcrook`, `Smith`, `Feeble`, and across it `Boardslide` or `Lipslide`; a lip trick from `HawkLip` (`Nose Stall` with nothing held); a manual is a `Manual` or a `Nose Manual`. A button with nothing held does the Left slot's trick, the THPS convention, since THUG has no slot for it. Two taps of one direction then the button are THUG's double-tap tricks (`Sal Flip`, `Ollie North`; `Nosebluntslide`, `Bluntslide` on a rail). The same button again mid-trick is the extra (`ExtraTricks`): a `Kickflip` becomes a `Double Kickflip` then a `Triple Kickflip`, a `Melon` a `Method`, each with THUG's points, replacing the trick in the combo; a flip's extra turns for another `FLIP_TIME`, so it needs the air. Every trick's points are THUG's own, from its trick scripts (`airtricks.q`, `grindscripts.q`, `manualtricks.q`, `liptricks.q`, `walltricks.q`, `tricks.q`): flips 100 and the hardflip 300, grabs 300 and the stalefish 350, grinds 100 and the slides 200, manuals 100, lip tricks 300 to 600, a wallride 200, a wallplant 750, a transfer or an acid drop 250, a revert 100, a plain ollie 75. A flip takes 0.45 s and landing before it is done is a bail; a grab lasts while the button is held; time on a grind or a manual earns nothing, as in THUG, the trick linked on the far side is the reward. The yaw turned in the air is counted on landing to the nearest half turn with THUG's `spin_count_slop` of 60 degrees and multiplies the trick of that air (or an ollie added for it) by `SPIN_MULT_VALUES`: one and a half for a 180, two for a 360, up to three and a half; the HUD reads `FS 360 Kickflip`, and an ollie's odd half turns land fakie so their side is swapped. A landing square across the roll is a bail. A trick done again in the run is worth `DEPREC_VALUES` of itself: 100, 75, 50, 25 then 10 per cent, counting banked combos and this one. Every trick since the last clean landing is the combo, worth its points times its count; a clean landing banks it after a quarter second's grace, in which a manual, a grind or a revert (`focus` or `shoot` within 0.3 s of a vert landing, THUG's L2 and R2) carries it on. A bail loses it.
- **The special meter and special tricks** (`Score::Update`, `Score::Trigger`, `Score::Bail`, `CSkater::GetStat`, `SpecialTricks` in `airtricks.q`, `skater_profile.q`): the meter is fed by every point the combo grows by, as the skater scores rather than on the landing, drains fifty points a second, and lights at three thousand; lit, it drains two hundred a second and goes out when empty, and a bail empties it. While it is lit every stat is three higher (8 of 10 instead of 5: the push, the top speed, the ollie and the spin all read further along their `physics.q` ranges, through `Skateboard._stat`) and the special tricks open: two directions tapped then the button within 400 ms (THUG's `TripleInOrder`), the three slots THUG gives a created skater: Left, Right, Flip is a `Kickflip Underflip` (1000), Right, Down, Grab a `McTwist` (5000), Right, Down, Grind a `Tailblock Slide` (500). The `HUD/SpecialBar` shows the meter and `HUD/SpecialLabel` says SPECIAL while it is lit.
- **Lip tricks** (`got_rail`'s lip branch, `do_lip_physics`, `HandleLipOllieDirection`): a rail found while rising in vert air with Grind held, off a wall steeper than `LipRampVertAngle` (68.5 degrees), is a lip trick, not a grind: the skater stops dead on the coping, named by the direction held (`Axle Stall`, `Disaster`, `Blunt To Fakie`, `Rock To Fakie`, `Nose Stall`), balancing on Up/Down with THUG's `LipParams` (four times a manual's instability, so a stall is short); the pop drops them back into the pipe from where they really were (`m_pre_lip_pos`), with `Lip_along_jump_speed` along the coping if Left or Right is held, or jumps over the deck at `Lip_side_jump_speed` if Up is held; a bail falls back in. The camera drops into the pipe below the coping and looks up (`lip_trick_tilt`, `lip_trick_above`).
- **Wall rides and wallplants** (`check_for_wallride`, `do_wallride_physics`, `check_for_wallplant`): a wall in the `wallride` group met in the air with Grind held (or pressed within `Wall_Ride_Triangle_Window`), `Wall_Ride_Delay` after the last ride, with at least `Wall_Ride_Min_Speed` along it and no squarer than `Wall_Ride_Max_Incident_Angle`, is ridden: the velocity is turned into the wall's plane with its speed kept, the model stands on the wall, `Wall_Ride_Gravity` pulls down it and the ride bends toward straight down by `Wall_Ride_Turn_Speed` a frame until it points well down; sideways speed is kept so the ride drifts; when the wall ends under the board the skater is pushed 18 in off it into the air; Ollie leaves it with `Wall_Ride_Jump_Out_Speed` and `Wall_Ride_Jump_Up_Speed`. Any vertical wall hit at least `Physics_Wallplant_Min_Approach_Angle` square, `Physics_Min_Wallplant_Height` off the ground, with Ollie pressed within a quarter second, is a wallplant: the horizontal speed is reflected and cut by `Physics_Wallplant_Speed_Loss` (never below `_Min_Exit_Speed`), the climb becomes `_Vertical_Exit_Speed`, the skater is set `_Distance_From_Wall` off the wall and frozen there for `_Duration`, and no other wallplant for a second. Both go into the combo.
- **Spine transfers and acid drops** (`maybe_spine_transfer`, `look_for_transfer_target`, `maybe_acid_drop`, `enter_acid_drop`, `calculate_time_to_reach_height`): rising in vert air with a spine button held (the revert buttons, THUG's L2 and R2), the board steps over the deck from 10 to 500 in dropping a long ray at each step for the first vert face (normal within 45 degrees of horizontal) that is not the wall it came up; one facing back within 30 degrees is a spine, any other a hip (Left or Right widens the search that way); if the faces are not opposite or the deck is wider than two feet the velocity is made straight up first; the time to come back down to the far lip is solved from the climb and `Physics_Air_Gravity` (`time_to_reach_height`), the horizontal speed set to cross in that time, refused if a wide spine needs more speed than the skater has, and held until the line across to the target is clear of the deck. In plain air with a spine button held, the board scans ahead along the travel for a vert face whose front is the way it travels, requires the drop to be reachable, at least `Physics_Acid_Drop_Min_Air_Time` away and clear along a two-leg path (raising the target 24 in at a time while something blocks it), gives a drop off an edge `Physics_Acid_Drop_Pop_Speed` of pop (twice it at most) and pulls the skater in on the same crossing speed. Either way, on coming down through the target's height the crossing speed is dropped and the skater is in vert air on the far face, tracked in its plane, to land on it and revert out. Each is in the combo (`Spine Transfer`, `Hip Transfer`, `Acid Drop`).
- **Camera** (`CSkaterCameraComponent::Update`): runs in the physics tick after the board has moved the skater. A target frame is built from the way the skater travels (the velocity, flattened four fifths against the ground and fully in the air) and the skater's up (the smoothed ground normal, so the view pitches up a transition with them; world up in plain air), tilted down a little and twenty degrees more in the air; the camera's frame turns toward it by a fixed fraction per sixtieth of a second (`slerp`, corrected for the frame rate the way THUG's `GetTimeAdjustedSlerp` does) with a limiter on how far it may swing in one frame; a tripod lags the skater by `lerp_xz` and `lerp_y`; the camera sits `behind` the frame and `above` the skater from the tripod and is aimed at the true skater, so the lag reads as distance. In vert air the forward is straight down, the tripod rides on the skater and the frame's up is the wall's normal, so the camera goes overhead with them and watches them come back down the same wall, then swings back behind them faster for ten frames after the landing. On a rail it zooms to `grind_zoom` and rolls with the balance; a trick started in vert air zooms it to `big_air_trick_zoom` until the vert air ends (`CalculateZoom`). A ray from the focus and two side feelers keep it out of walls. `field_of_view` is vertical, 55 degrees for THUG's 72 horizontal.

- **Skitching** (`maybe_skitch`, `move_to_skitch_point`, `SkitchParams`, the `Skitch` script): Up held for `Skitch_Hold_Time` (200 ms) on the ground within `Skitch_Max_Distance` (120 in) of a vehicle's skitch point takes it; the skater takes the vehicle's velocity (`skitch_speed_match`) and is pulled to the point at `skitch_suck_speed` (200 in/s) on top, faces the way it travels, and balances on Left/Right with THUG's `SkitchParams` (half a manual's gravity and instability, the gentlest meter), worth `Skitchin` 500. Down, the meter's end or a vehicle that gets away lets go without a bail (THUG's `SkitchOut`) and the skater rolls on at that speed; a pop lets go into the air with it. A second's grace before the next grab stands in for THUG's let-go animation.
- **Walking** (`CWalkComponent`, `SwitchControl_Trigger`, `maybe_jump_to_acid_drop`, `maybe_in_air_acid_drop`): THUG's L1 + R1 gets off the board with it in hand and back on again anywhere, the air included, where the skater lands on it; a spine button on foot jumps at `acid_drop_jump_velocity` (400 in/s) onto the board to drop into a vert face ahead, and in the air drops in from there. The walking itself is the Player's own (run, jump, climb); only the transitions are the board's.

THUG's pro skaters' own slots and the rest of its trick names are not here; the tables at the top of `skate_tricks.gd` are where they go, with the points from THUG's trick scripts.
